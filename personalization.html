Architecture & Design

I designed the audience segmentation strategy for this project, defining three distinct target customer personas. I mapped out how a single brand's messaging, imagery, and product layout should dynamically shift to maximize conversion rates for each psychological profile.

The Logic
Behind the
Shift

Dynamic personalization isn't just changing copy — it's restructuring the entire emotional contract between brand and visitor. Each persona triggers a different psychological pitch: urgency for the commuter, achievement for the athlete, identity for the creator. The product stays the same. The story changes everything.

01 — Signal

Psychographic Trigger

A URL parameter, cookie, or CRM tag identifies the visitor's persona cluster. This can come from a past purchase, quiz funnel, ad click, or firmographic match — all without changing the page URL.

02 — Map

Content Swap Layer

A persona data object holds every variable: headline, subtext, color palette, product set, CTA copy, and social proof. A single function call replaces the entire visual state in under 400ms.

03 — Convert

Higher Relevance → Higher CVR

Personalized landing experiences consistently outperform generic ones by 35–80% in conversion rate. The investment is in mapping content to audience — the payoff is in every session that feels intentional.
