The Logic of
Speed-to-Lead

In modern marketing operations, the time between a lead submitting a form and receiving a response is the single biggest lever on conversion rate. This pipeline simulates the logic I apply in real campaigns: standardize the data, score the intent, and route instantly — so the right person always gets the right lead at the right time.

By automating text cleansing and sentiment detection, teams eliminate manual triage. High-intent signals like pricing inquiries or urgency language trigger direct sales handoffs, while softer leads flow into nurture sequences that build trust over time.
